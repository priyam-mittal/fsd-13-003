Our Journey

From R&D Lab to Global Flagship

2018 — The Spark

Founding of Apex Mobile

Former silicon architects and aerospace engineers assemble in San Jose, California, with a vision to revolutionize smartphone tactile design and raw processing efficiency.

2021 — Breakthrough Silicon

Unveiling Bionic Neural Engine

Apex completes its proprietary 3nm coprocessor architecture, setting new records in real-time ray tracing and energy conservation per watt.

2024 — Aura OS Launch

Redefining Mobile Interaction

Aura OS 1.0 debuts, introducing contextual fluid widgets and AI-assisted photo reconstruction, winning the International UX Design Grand Prix.

2026 — Present Era

Aura Phone X Launch

The culmination of 8 years of hardware and software perfection: Grade 5 Titanium chassis, 200MP Quad Optics, and 100W dual charging.
